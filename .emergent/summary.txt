-   **users:** {email, role, wallet_balance, free_minutes, ...}

-   **chat_messages:** {room_id, sender_id, text, image_url} 
-   **wallet_transactions:** {user_id, amount, type, status} 
-   **call_history:** {user_id, provider_id, duration, cost, status} 
-   **system_config:** {key, value (encrypted)}
